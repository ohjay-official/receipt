# Railway, studied 15 Nov 2026

Live page: https://railway.com. Playwright, Chrome, 1440×900 and 412×839. The page is about 12,500px on desktop and 13,400px on a phone. Body background is `rgb(19, 17, 28)`. Type is Inter. Section elements themselves are transparent. The colour lives on the page, not on each band.

## One canvas

Nothing between the hero and the footer changes the page colour. A section is a block of space on the same night field. The hero is a scene (stars, clouds) that darkens into that field. The product window rises out of the bottom of the hero and is still there as the next sentence starts, so the scroll does not feel like a new page.

Later bands do the same. A headline and one sentence sit on the field. Under them, a large product surface (the project canvas, charts, a log) uses a slightly deeper neutral, with a hairline, not a new hue. Charts use a little colour inside the product, not on the page.

The footer is the same field. There is no rule above it. The split-flap numbers and the link columns sit in the same dark. The only edge is the rounded corner of a product frame, and that frame belongs to the content.

## Rhythm

Each band is the same shape:

1. A short label, sometimes with a small icon.
2. A large left-aligned headline, weight around 500, not a shout.
3. One or two sentences, secondary colour, with a text link at the end.
4. Then the visual, wider than the text and tall enough to be the section.

Desktop bands run from about 760px to 1,700px because the product picture is large, not because of empty padding. On a phone the same order stacks, and the product frame stays wide.

## Bleed

The hero scene continues behind the first product window. The next section’s copy starts while the previous window is still leaving the viewport. Visuals are not boxed into a card with a different page colour around them. They sit on the field and run close to the next headline.

## Type and padding

One sans for everything measured. Headlines are the only large type. Body stays near 16–18px and secondary grey. Section padding is generous and even, on the order of 80–120px of field between the end of one visual and the next headline. There is no alternating light band.

## What Receipt takes from this

The structure: one ink field from the bottom of the jet to the last footer line. Sections separated by space, not by borders or paper slabs. Product UI (a receipt, a run, a log) floats on that field. The footer uses the same ink and has no line above it.

What stays Receipt’s: the words, the real pull-request ids, the paper only where a receipt or a tear-off slip is an object, and stamp red on the scanner, the stamp, and the one figure that matters.
